    <p>I'm Alonso Astroza. I lead corporate partnerships at the <a href="https://ingenieria.udd.cl/persona/alonso-astroza-tagle/">Data Science Institute at Universidad del Desarrollo</a>, where I work on connecting research, industry, and applied AI projects.</p>
    <p>I also founded <a href="https://iadevs.cl/">IADevs</a>, a community for AI engineers in Chile that organizes in-person events. More recently, I created <a href="https://codexparatodos.com/">Codex para Todos</a> ("Codex for Everyone"), an independent Spanish-speaking community focused on helping people use <a href="https://openai.com/codex/">OpenAI Codex</a> in practical workflows.</p>
    <p>Right now, most of my work sits at the intersection of LLMs, humor, local culture, and speech. That includes <a href="https://datarisas.cl/">DataRisas</a>, an explorable dataset of Chilean humor, <a href="https://github.com/idsudd/tricahue">Tricahue</a>, a speech modeling effort for Chilean Spanish, and my role as a technical ambassador for <a href="https://site.kotoba.tech/">Kotoba Technologies</a>.</p>
    <p>This site is where I collect notes, experiments, writing, and projects around those themes.</p>
